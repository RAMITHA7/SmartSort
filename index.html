Our impact

Small sorting decisions. Better cities.

SmartSort is designed as an educational prototype supporting awareness around waste segregation and responsible waste management.

The project connects with UN Sustainable Development Goal 11, particularly Target 11.6, which focuses on reducing the environmental impact of cities, including through better municipal waste management.

Transparency

Built with responsible guidance

SmartSort is an educational hackathon prototype. Its classifications are based on the prototype dataset and are intended to help users understand waste categories.

Actual waste-management requirements, collection systems and recycling options may vary by local authority. Items requiring special handling should follow applicable local guidance and authorised collection channels.
